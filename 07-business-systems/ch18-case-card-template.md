# Case Card Template (Chapter 18)

One card per real event, written from your notes before any chapter is drafted.

```
CASE CARD  C__                 Chapter: ____

Title:       [one line, what happened]
What:        [the event, with real dates and numbers]
Why:         [the cause, as far as you know it]
Result:      [what changed afterwards]
Lesson:      [one sentence]
Artifact:    [template or checklist it produced]
Source:      [your note or record. NEVER PRINTED]
Remove:      [names, prices, addresses, IDs to strip]
Unsure:      [anything you can't confirm. Writers must
             not state it as fact.]
```
