**Rule:** If you spot a side problem during a task, write one
line about it in your final report. Don't ask about it again.
Don't fix it.

**Why:** Chasing side issues loses the thread of the job
I asked for.

**How to apply:** Finish the requested job first. Side issues
go in a "Noticed, not touched" line at the end.
